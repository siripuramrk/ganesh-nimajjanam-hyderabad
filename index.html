Rules that apply on immersion day

These are administrative and judicial decisions, so they are listed here in full rather than buried in the data. Rules change by order — see the note at the foot of this page.

  1. Tank Bund road-side immersion is banned

    Immersion from the Tank Bund road side is not permitted. GHMC and Hyderabad Police installed warning banners and a roughly 10-foot railing along Tank Bund to stop direct immersion from the road, citing a High Court order and traffic congestion. The ban applies to the Tank Bund side, not to Hussain Sagar as a whole.

  2. Plaster of Paris (PoP) idols may not enter Hussain Sagar or any natural water body

    PoP idols must go to artificial “baby ponds” only. The Telangana High Court (2021, reiterated in 2023) barred direct PoP immersion in Hussain Sagar and other natural water bodies; the Supreme Court allowed a one-time exception for 2021 only, on the state’s undertaking to build alternatives. Baby ponds are provided at Necklace Road and Sanjeevaiah Park among other sites.

  3. Approved Hussain Sagar immersion points: NTR Marg, Necklace Road, People’s Plaza

    Cranes, platforms and equipment are installed at these three points. Immersion from the Tank Bund road side is not permitted.

  4. Idol height cap: 16 feet

    Announced by Hyderabad Police to reduce risk from overhead electrical lines, cable and internet wires, bridges, road clearances and crane movement. Organisers moving large idols must coordinate with traffic police and avoid peak hours.

  5. Recommended: eco-friendly clay idols

    Follow Telangana State Pollution Control Board conditions. Clay and eco-friendly idols may use designated lake-side crane points; PoP idols may not enter natural water bodies at all.

Getting around

Expect the procession to dominate the Old City corridor and the lakefront through the day.

Public transport on 25 September

Metro Rail services are expected to run until about 1 a.m. Overnight MMTS services connect Nampally, Lakdi-ka-pul, Khairatabad, Sanjeevaiah Park, James Street and Begumpet. Extra buses and goods vehicles are arranged.

Road works freeze

No digging, drilling or cable-laying until immersion ends. All ongoing road, nala and slab works on main and feeder immersion routes had to be finished before 14–15 September. Loose and hanging electricity, cable and internet wires are being removed along the route.

What to avoid

  • Do not plan an immersion from the Tank Bund road side — it is banned.
  • Do not take a Plaster of Paris (PoP) idol into Hussain Sagar or any natural water body; baby ponds are the only route for PoP idols.
  • Keep idols within the 16 feet height cap and avoid peak hours when moving large idols.
  • Expect heavy congestion around Charminar, M.J. Market and the Telugu Thalli Junction / Flyover approach to the lake.
